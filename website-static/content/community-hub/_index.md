### Community Hub

At the Aisling Centre we provide a hub for the entire community. It is a welcoming and inclusive environment. There are a number of groups who meet up on a regular basis whether you are struggling with addiction, grief or mental health problems. There are also group catch ups of carers, parents and their young children.

We also have numerous [workshops and classes](/community-hub/workshops-classes/), which provide a creative and emotional outlet for people as well as entertainment.
