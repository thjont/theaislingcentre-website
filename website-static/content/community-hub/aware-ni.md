**AWARE NI Peer Support Groups**

*Open to anyone 18*+ *affected by depression, anxiety, bipolar or any mental health issues, including their relatives or carers.*

Living with depression, anxiety or bipolar can be very isolating. AWARE’s support groups provide an opportunity to meet others going through similar experiences as you. This peer-led support can help you understand your illness, allow you to speak freely about how you are feeling and make you realise that you are not alone. Support group members share information, discuss options and support each other to move forward.

The group meets in the Aisling Centre on Tuesday evenings, 7.00pm – 8.30pm. Meetings are free of charge and there is no need to book a place to attend.

For further information, please email [info@aware-ni.org](mailto:info@aware-ni.org) or call 028 9035 7820. To find your nearest support group, visit [https://aware-ni.org/support-groups/](https://aware-ni.org/support-groups/)
