### Annual Events

### Cuilcagh Trek for World Suicide Prevention Day

For two years now Aisling Centre has held an event to mark World Suicide Prevention Day on 10th of September. Members of the public and staff of Aisling Centre make the trek up Cuilcagh Mountain at the crack of dawn.

[More Information](/well-being/annual-events/cuilcagh-trek/)

### Hope, Healing, Growth

The Hope Healing Growth event has become an established part of Aisling Centre’s Events’ calendar. The event aims to give a positive start to the year by providing an inspirational and exciting programme of speakers and contributors.

[More Information](/well-being/annual-events/hope-healing-growth/)
