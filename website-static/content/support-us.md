### Support Us

### 100 people attend counselling at Aisling Centre every week

All donations gifted to Aisling Centre are used to support your local counselling and well-being services

Even a small donation will make a big difference.

***“My counsellor was very supportive and kind and helped me through one of the most difficult times in my life.  Where I was completely overwhelmed before, I am now feeling more positive and optimistic and I have a more self compassion too”***

***“I felt valued, listened to and understood“***

***“Before it was all darkness with no way out”***

**W****ays you can fundraise for us:**

There are many ways in which you can raise valuable funds for Aisling Centre.  Some suggestions are:

- Nominate us as your workplace charity
- Organise an event
- Undertake a personal challenge such as a sponsored walk
- Donations in lieu of birthday gifts or wedding favours etc
- Leave a gift in your will

If you wish to fundraise for us please get in touch so that we can help you maximise your efforts.

Remember to set up your event on Local Giving [here](https://localgiving.org/charity/aislingcentre/)

### Leave a lasting legacy for future generations

A simple way to help local people with mental health issues is to leave a gift in your Will.

Making a will can be daunting process so please get in touch with your solicitor if you wish to leave a gift to a charity.
