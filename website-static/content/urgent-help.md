### Urgent Help

Aisling Centre is open 9.00am – 5.00pm, Monday to Friday.

If you need help outside of these hours or need emergency support, the contact details below may be helpful

### Emergency Contact Numbers

**Lifeline:** 0808 808 8000

**Emergency Services:** 999

**GP Out of Hours Service:** 028 7186 5195

**Samaritans:** 116 123

**Gateway team:** 028 7131 4090

**NSPCC:** 0808 800 5000

**Childline:** 0800 1111

If you are concerned about a child or young person, your first point of contact should be the Gateway Team.
