### Our Book Recommendations

![](/images/2023/10/61b1hP0XSL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

From Sunday Times bestselling author Fearne Cotton, this is the handbook for modern life we all need. Including expert advice, ideas to put into practice and activities to complete. Quiet seeks out ways to help you tune out the negative backchat that holds you back.

![](/images/2023/10/owl.jpg)

Filled with gentle humour and heart-warming illustrations, this uplifting story about finding the courage to face your fears has been beloved of parents and children for decades – and its message of resilience is as timely as ever.

![](/images/2023/10/71Ey8D2uQrL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

Filled with concrete techniques to turn passivity into assertiveness. Dr. Susan Jeffers, teaches you how to stop negative thinking patterns and re-educate your mind to think more positively.

![](/images/2023/10/61fkOjKgABL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

Oprah shares her experiences and insights on overcoming hardship and how she used that strength to forge a path toward reaching her full potential.

![](/images/2023/10/61h6HUFTXL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

From the popular Sunday World columnist this book will help whether you need advice, consolation, guidance in prayer or just a good laugh.

![](/images/2023/10/9781838087128.jpg)

This is a heartfelt story from Jonny McCambridge; a Northern Irish journalist. An honest account of his attempt to overcome the mental health demons which tormented him for decades and brought him dangerously close to the conclusion that life simply wasn’t worth living anymore.

![](/images/2023/10/71tzbaWNwpL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

A practical guide to the issues parents face in raising sons–including sex, violence, homework, sports, the Internet, and more–and how to best aid boys’ development from birth to manhood.

![](/images/2023/10/81PdkxXJObL._AC_UF8941000_QL80_.jpg)

Four unusual friends share truths about life as they search for a home. The central message is about the importance of kindness, and that love exists in unexpected places.
