### Embracing Change: Thriving Through Perimenopause & Menopause

Aisling Centre held a very successful menopause and perimenopause information event on 29th April in partnership with Fermanagh & Omagh District Council.

Speakers included:  **Dr Andrea Latimer** – a GP based in Fermanagh who specialises in Women’s Health and is a British Menopause Society accredited Menopause Specialist  **Angela Treacy** –  mental health practitioner with over 25 years experience.  Angela has completed the British Menopause Society Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) training **Roisin Hillman & Karen Pateman** – representing Menopause Together, Northern Ireland’s first menopause charity.  To open the Powerpoint slides of resources and further information please click [here](/files/2024/05/Menopause-resources.pptx).
